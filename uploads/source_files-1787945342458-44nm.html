A different kind of December

Christmas can be full of beautiful things and still leave us feeling like we missed something.

Every year we decorate, shop, plan, gather, and count down. The season can become so full that the Person at the center of it quietly slips to the edges.

Unwrapping Advent is a 25-day devotional created to help you slow down and look again. Each day “unwraps” a gift found in who Christ is and what He has done—gifts like hope, promise, faith, grace, peace, forgiveness, and His presence.

The journey begins with God’s promises, moves toward the arrival of Christ, follows His nearness into ordinary life, and looks ahead to the cross, resurrection, and the future secured in Him. The goal is not to manufacture a better Christmas mood. It is to receive what lasts.

About the author

Dan Case

“I am not a pastor. I do not lead a church. I am simply a man trying to follow Jesus in the steady noise of everyday life.”

Dan has spent more than twenty-three years working in advertising as a creative professional. He is a husband and father of four, and his writing grows out of the intersection of Scripture, ordinary family life, creative work, and a desire to pay closer attention to what God is doing beneath the surface.

Unwrapping Advent grew from his family’s own December tradition: gathering together, lighting candles, reading Scripture, singing carols, and learning that slowing down can steady a season that otherwise moves too quickly.

His hope for the book is simple—that it gives readers permission to slow down, listen, notice what they might otherwise walk past, and turn their hearts again toward Christ.
